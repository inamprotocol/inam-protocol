INAM × PayPal / USDC · pay on proof

An AI agent can pay, in PayPal or USDC, only against a verified receipt.

The payment is held first: a PayPal authorization, or a signed USDC transfer (x402) the buyer keeps unsent. The buyer agent captures only when the seller's signed INAM receipt matches what was actually delivered and held; anything else releases the hold and the owner is never charged. Same receipt, same reputation on either rail.

Check the seller. INAM identity and track record before any payment call. Unknown or revoked: refused.
Hold, don't pay. PayPal: an AUTHORIZE order the owner approves. USDC: an EIP-3009 authorization signed and kept. Nothing moves.
Deliver with a receipt. The seller signs an INAM receipt naming the delivery hash and the hold.
Capture on proof. Match: countersign, then capture (PayPal) or settle (x402). Mismatch: void or discard. Code decides, the AI explains.
